About

Learn more about Modulogical, the ideas behind it, and the project that is being built around it.

Modulogical is an AI ecosystem focused on modularity, allowing AI systems to be extended through independently created components.

The ecosystem is being built around Cloudflare hosting and includes concepts such as personalities, themes, modules, databases, workflows and agents.

Modulogical is an independent project created and developed by _____.

The project combines interests in artificial intelligence, software development, and the design of modular systems.

The long-term idea behind Modulogical is to make AI systems more extensible, giving developers the ability to create, combine and share components rather than building every capability from scratch.

Engine Forge is the creation side of the ecosystem, where developers can build components that can become part of Modulogical.

Modulogical is being developed using a combination of web technologies, serverless infrastructure, databases, and AI inference systems.

The underlying technology will continue to evolve as the project develops.
